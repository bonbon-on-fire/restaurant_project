# Chef notes — 2026-08-06 (recipe behaviour / production)

A fuller reading of the chef's **MOCK MENU NOTES** page. The 2026-07-24 review
captured the day-placement and cold-soup rules; this pass captures the
**recipe-behaviour / production** half that was missing. All **chef-stated**;
outranks the inferred patterns in `data/menus/PATTERNS.md`.

## Corrections (fold into PATTERNS.md)

- **Four-day work week, Monday–Thursday.** Friday is service-only — no plate or
  soup production; Thursday's late-afternoon soups serve Friday. Corrects the
  "five days of a week" phrasing in the texture rule. (per_day_balance)
- **Sandwiches = one végé (hummus pomme brie noix) OR texture (banh-mi) + one
  fish**, in a normal week. (per_day_balance)
- **Weather does not move sandwich production** — only the social calendar does.
  (weather_temp)
- **Sandwiches made Tuesday morning after the soups**; a Monday/Tuesday holiday
  halves it (one sort) or spreads it over two days. (calendar)
- **Sauces made mainly Tuesdays**, except the lasagna marinara (made the day
  before lasagna production). (calendar)
- **Rutabaga pomme sirop d'érable potage → cold months; biweekly in maple season
  (Mar–Apr); poor summer seller.** (calendar)
- **Soupe Coréenne (Korean) → all-year top seller, near-weekly; may run 3 weeks
  straight then pause a week, or appear twice in one week on demand/produce.**
  (rotation)
- **Soupe Champignon estragon (Mushroom tarragon) → biweekly best seller, but
  laborious → capped at once per week.** (rotation)
- **Mole verde soup → discontinued.** (rotation)
- **Burgers → demand-driven, no rotation pattern**; made when a variety runs low.
  Four condiment varieties (mono fuko, à l'aneth, légumes grillés et pesto,
  chipotle aioli) rotate every ~1.5 weeks. Refines the inferred "warm-season
  burgers" read — the warm-month clustering is a demand effect, not a scheduling
  rule. (rotation)

## Taxonomy overrides (fold into menus-tag)

- **Texture dish = principal ingredient is végé chicken or végé beef** (both soy-
  and shiitake-based). Falafels are végé, not texture.
- **Hummus pomme brie noix** = the standard végé sandwich; **banh-mi** = the
  standard texture sandwich.
- **Mole verde** = discontinued; do not schedule.

## Production-quantity themes (captured, NOT modelled)

`PATTERNS.md` tracks *what dish goes where*, not *how much is made*. These
quantities are recorded here until a demand/volume model exists.

### Soups

Every day begins with one potage and one soupe. In the **colder months**, high
demand pushes soup production up by three to four extra soups; **Thursdays almost
always make four soups** (two in the morning, two in the late afternoon) — the
last two serve Friday in the restaurant and are packaged for the store's
prêt-à-manger. In the **hotter months** production is cut (except cold soups):
Tuesday and Wednesday often make only one soup or potage and reheat the previous
day's, to avoid oversupply in the prêt-à-manger fridge and the resulting **−20 %
near-expiry markdown**.

### Sandwiches

Usual quantity **18 of each**; each sandwich has a **5–7 day expiry**. If a
previous week left too many unsold, the next week is cut: either **one sort only**
or **both sorts capped at 12 each**.

### Sauces

The lasagna marinara batch yields **two lasagna batches** (half is frozen for the
next production). The sauce is sold as prêt-à-manger only: split into **15–17
pasta portions** (4 oz of sauce over gluten-free pasta) plus **6–7 × 500 ml
containers**.

### Burgers

Each burger recipe yields **~200 burgers**: **¼ reserved for the restaurant**, the
rest frozen and packaged sous-vide in **4-packs**.

### Falafels

Made according to demand as the complete dish, or as **sous-vide falafels in bags
of 8** (already noted 2026-07-24).

## Data note

Some items may be missing from the rotation simply because they are **new** and
have not yet accumulated appearances — absence from the rotation table is not
evidence a dish was dropped (except Mole verde, which is explicitly discontinued).
