# Chef notes — 2026-08-09 (per-menu dish corrections)

A third reading of the chef's **MOCK MENU NOTES** page, capturing the
**per-menu, dish-specific corrections** on the five generated August mock menus
(warm-sunny week, heat-wave week, back-to-school week). All **chef-stated**;
outranks the inferred patterns in `data/menus/PATTERNS.md`.

The paste's opening "cold rainy autumn week" section and its closing
"Recipe Behaviour / production" section were already folded from
`2026-07-24.md` and `2026-08-06.md` respectively, and are **not** repeated here.

## Corrections (fold into PATTERNS.md)

### Weekly composition (per_day_balance)

- **One chicken + one végé-beef texture per week** (texture = shiitaké-based
  végé beef).
- **At most two cabbage salads per week**; the KM / cabbage / beetroot staples
  are deliberately varied.
- **Exactly one carrot salad per week** — Trio racine, carrot-celeriac-almond,
  or carrot-orange.
- **Conditional greens:** green taboulé only when no falafels that week;
  green-bean salad only when salmon ginger + its child sandwich + green-bean
  hummus are all present (uses a full case of green beans at quality).

### Weather / temperature (weather_temp)

- **Cold soup only on a 2–3 day hot streak** — one hot day is not enough
  (refines the ≥24 °C rule from 2026-07-24).
- **Chipotle lentil soup → cold-winter soup, not a big seller.**
- **Pico de Gallo's Thursday slot is weather-/surplus-driven** — warm weather or
  a tomato surplus (refines "Thursday, never Monday").

### Rotation (pairing, alternation, discontinuation)

- **Ratatouille ↔ lasagna rarely share a week** — the two large-format
  tomato-sauce dishes alternate, end-of-summer through late spring.
- **Mushroom risotto ↔ paella never share a week**; each biweekly, 2–3 weeks
  apart.
- **Salmon ginger (60 portions) pairs with salmon pesto (~44 portions)** in the
  same week.
- **Haddock mango coconut ragout for variety** — no plain/medium haddock added
  alongside.
- **Asian/spicy soups (Coréenne, green tom yum, phõ, peanut Thai)** are the best
  sellers, rotated for variety; **savoury non-spicy options are always kept too.**
- **Orange fennel salad** is mainly the accompaniment to codfish croquettes;
  rare as a standalone salad.
- **One poké per week**; three flavours rotate: salmon gravlax, poulet végé
  coréenne, salmon ginger.
- **Green lime hummus discontinued → green bean hummus**; vary hummus colours.
- **Gravlax retired to key events only** (Mother's/Father's Day, Christmas,
  Easter, high summer) → **Moqueca** takes its slot.
- **Fish soups (Chaudrée, Espagnol, Bouillabaisse) on hold** until the economy
  recovers; when back, one/week in colder months only.
- **Crémé de tomate** uses up surplus store tomatoes.
- **Rémoulade à l'orange → biweekly, alternating with Trio racine**, but off when
  jerk chicken is on the menu.
- **Two composé salads alternate biweekly: tuna salad ↔ végé chicken salad**
  (végé chicken salad newly added).
- **Ham quiche → forestier (mushroom) quiche.**
- **Chou-fleur Général Tao and Romesco appear when cauliflower is cheap**
  (price-driven).
- **Wraps** sell well during the school year when sandwiches sell out — tuna
  salad, végé chicken salad, hummus pomme brie noix.
- **Stand-by dishes** fill open slots: ratatouille, a 7th salad, one salade
  composé, one of croustade/medium fish.

### Calendar

- **Back-to-school weeks → reduced production, favour staples.**

## Taxonomy overrides (fold into menus-tag)

- **Champignon estragon** = soupe (not potage).
- **Salade Bandu** = composé (not salade de jour).
- **Taboulé** = salade de jour (not composé).
- **Brocoli crémeuse** = salade de jour / side (not composé).
- **Provençal** and **Crumble de pois chiches** = good composé salads.
- **Végé chicken salad** = composé (alternates biweekly with tuna salad).
- **Satay sauce** = accompaniment/condiment, not a pasta sauce.
- **Orange fennel salad** = accompaniment to codfish croquettes.
- **Green lime hummus** = discontinued → green bean hummus.
- **Gravlax** = key-events only (Moqueca replaces it in normal weeks).
- **Salmon parent → child-sandwich recipes:** Salmon pesto (artichaut, poivrons,
  zucchini), Salmon Gingembre, Salmon Cajun each spawn a child sandwich.
- **Poulet jerk** = use the végé jerk version, with coconut rice + plantains.

## Menu-construction mechanisms (stand-by & substitution)

`PATTERNS.md` models *what dish goes where*, not the swap logic the chef uses to
flex a week. Recorded here for the recommender:

- **Stand-by pool** — ratatouille, a 7th salad, one salade composé, and one of
  croustade/medium fish are held in reserve to fill open slots.
- **Heat-wave substitutions** — ratatouille → Hilairoise salade; salmon ginger →
  salmon croustade; poulet satay → poké coréenne poulet végé; satay sauce →
  shrimp & scallop sauce; green lime hummus → green bean hummus.
- **Back-to-school substitutions** — ratatouille → Chèvre et le chou; paëlla →
  Chou-fleur Général Tao (cauliflower price permitting); poké → Poulet Thaï; jerk
  rémoulade → Légumes braisées et texture shiitaké (végé beef); the soup lineup
  leans on Champignon estragon, Thaï arachide, Légumes amandes coco, Ribollita,
  and Harira; satay sauce → Romesco (cauliflower price permitting); ham quiche →
  forestier quiche.

## Production-quantity themes (captured, NOT modelled)

Deferred like the other volume notes until a demand/volume model exists:

- **Salmon ginger** is a large recipe at **60 portions**; its weekly partner
  **salmon pesto** runs about **44 portions**.

## Data note

Several dishes here are new or price-/event-gated (Moqueca, green bean hummus,
forestier quiche, Chou-fleur Général Tao, Romesco). Absence from the rotation
table is not evidence a dish was dropped — only Green lime hummus and Gravlax
(the latter only outside key events) are explicitly pulled from the normal
rotation, alongside the previously-noted Mole verde.
