## Comments and weather

MOCK week — a Montreal August unstable/thunderstorm week (Week 3 of 5): warm sunny
days (Mon 26 °C, Thu 27 °C, Fri 25 °C) interrupted by a cool stormy Tue (22 °C) and
a rainy Wed (21 °C). The week is built to **test the cold-soup direction rule**: the
chilled Cold Mélonccio soup appears ONLY on the hottest day (Thu, 27 °C), while the
cool stormy Tue/Wed lean on hot, hearty soups.

## Why this menu

### Strategy

The scenario is a classic Montreal August: a run of warm-to-hot sunny days broken
by a two-day thunderstorm/rain trough mid-week. The whole week is a light,
salad-forward summer menu (six salads + three composed salads, burgers and Pico in
play as warm-season items) — but the temperature *swing* is the real design driver.
The two cool days (Tue 22 °C, Wed 21 °C) are treated as demand-*broadening*, not
demand-narrowing: hot hearty soups lead, hearty mains (lasagna, paella) come out,
and salads still run. The three hot days (Mon/Thu/Fri, all ≥25 °C) go salad-lean,
and the single chilled cold soup is reserved for the hottest of them. The six
rotation staples all appear — Ham (as the quiche), Poutine, Kale mango cabbage,
Salmon pesto artichoke, Korean (soup), and Creamy broccoli (salad).

### Per-day notes

- **Monday (26 °C, sunny)** — Warm sunny open. Kale mango cabbage (KM) anchors
  Monday per the chef's placement rule; Poutine and the Ham quiche fill the
  Monday "full-fridge" role. Soup slot is a smooth potage (cream of tomato) plus a
  light brothy tom yum — warm but not hot enough to justify the cold soup, which is
  held back for Thursday. Pico de Gallo is deliberately absent (never on Monday).
- **Tuesday (22 °C, cloudy, thunderstorms)** — Cool storm day. Soups go hot and
  hearty (Korean, chipotle lentil, mushroom tarragon); no cold soup at 22 °C. First
  texture main of the week (veggie jerk chicken). Note the storm demand pattern: the
  day *of* a storm, clients tend to stay home, so this is a lean-into-hot-comfort day
  rather than a volume day.
- **Wednesday (21 °C, rainy)** — Coolest day. Hearty hot soups lead, headlined by
  Fisherman's chowder (the chef's own note records chowder selling well in storm
  weather). No cold soup at 21 °C. No texture main — texture mains are spaced Tue/Thu,
  so Wednesday skips it.
- **Thursday (27 °C, humid, sunny)** — The hot pivot and **the** cold-soup day:
  Cold Mélonccio soup runs here and only here. Salad-lean and fresh (Greek, orange
  fennel), second texture main (veggie Thai chicken, spaced from Tuesday), and Pico
  de Gallo on its Thursday slot.
